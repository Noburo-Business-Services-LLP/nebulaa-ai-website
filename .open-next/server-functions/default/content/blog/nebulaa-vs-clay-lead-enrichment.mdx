# Nebulaa vs Clay: Two Approaches to AI-Powered Lead Generation

Clay has become one of the most talked-about tools in GTM circles over the last two years — and for good reason. If you want to build a hyper-personalised outbound list at scale, enriching each lead with 10+ data points and writing personalised first lines using AI, Clay is genuinely impressive.

But it's a power tool. And like most power tools, it requires a skilled operator, considerable setup time, and an understanding of what it can and can't do on its own.

Here's a full comparison of how Clay and Nebulaa approach AI-powered lead generation — and why for most Indian founders, the tools aren't really competing for the same use case.

## What Clay Does Well

Clay is a data enrichment and sequencing platform. At its core, you build a table of leads and then run a waterfall of data enrichment on each row: LinkedIn profile, company headcount, recent funding, tech stack, hiring signals, and more. The AI then uses all this data to write personalised outreach messages at scale.

The results can be impressive. A well-built Clay workflow can take 500 raw company names and produce 500 personalised outreach emails in a few hours — each one referencing something specific about the company that makes it feel handcrafted.

For sales teams at B2B SaaS companies targeting US or European enterprise buyers — Clay is a serious competitive advantage.

**Clay genuinely excels at**:
- Data enrichment at scale (pulling from 50+ sources)
- Waterfall enrichment (try source A, fall back to B, then C)
- Hyper-personalised first lines for cold email
- Building complex lead scoring logic
- Integrating with outbound tools like Instantly or Smartlead

## What Clay Doesn't Do

Clay is a data and enrichment tool. It doesn't:

- Make phone calls
- Send WhatsApp messages
- Have a conversation with a lead in real time
- Qualify a lead through a live interaction
- Generate or publish social content
- Learn your brand voice and post daily

The output of a Clay workflow is typically a CSV or a CRM-ready list, or it feeds into a separate sequencing tool. The "follow-up" in Clay's world is an email sequence — automated, but one-way. A lead replies, and now a human needs to pick it up.

For Indian SMB and D2C contexts, this creates a practical problem. Email open rates for Indian B2B are often 20–25%. Clay-powered cold email sequences hit low reply rates on Indian leads because the channel is less effective than in US markets. The enrichment is excellent; the delivery mechanism is suboptimal for the Indian context.

And the setup complexity is significant. Clay has a steep learning curve. Most founders who try to use it independently spend 2–3 weeks building their first workflow. Many hire a Clay specialist.

## Where Nebulaa's Pulsar Is Different

Pulsar doesn't enrich 500 leads with 10 data points each. It takes your leads and calls them.

This sounds simple. It's not. A real-time voice conversation with a lead — even an AI-powered one — does something no email sequence can do: it creates a two-way interaction in the moment. The lead responds. They ask questions. They reveal their actual situation. They either commit or they decline clearly.

The qualification signal from a 2-minute Pulsar call is far more reliable than the engagement signal from an opened email. By the end of the call, you know: is this lead ready to buy, not ready but interested, or clearly not a fit? That's information you can act on immediately.

Pulsar then routes accordingly: warm leads get a calendar link, nurture leads go into a WhatsApp sequence, and unfit leads are documented and archived.

## When to Use Clay

Use Clay when:

- You have a trained operator or are willing to invest 2–3 weeks in setup
- You're doing high-volume outbound to US or European markets where email still works
- Your sales team needs a constant stream of enriched, personalised leads fed into a sequencing tool
- You have a CRM and existing outbound stack that Clay can plug into
- Your ICP is well-defined and you need data enrichment to identify and prioritise the right accounts

## When to Use Nebulaa

Use Nebulaa when:

- You're selling in India where WhatsApp and calls outperform cold email
- You're a small team or solo founder without a dedicated SDR or Clay specialist
- You need leads to be qualified through actual conversations, not just tagged as "opened email"
- You want content (Gravity) and outreach (Pulsar) to work as one connected system
- Speed of setup matters — Pulsar is running your first campaign within a day

## Can You Use Both?

Yes, and some teams do. Clay for list building and enrichment → export to Pulsar for calling and qualification. This gives you Clay's enrichment quality with Pulsar's Indian-market-optimised outreach. It's a more advanced setup, but it works well for teams that have found PMF and are scaling outbound.

## Conclusion

Clay and Nebulaa are solving different problems in the lead generation chain. Clay enriches and personalises at scale. Nebulaa converses, qualifies, and follows up.

For the Indian founder who doesn't have a US-trained GTM team and needs results in the Indian market, Pulsar is the faster, more practical path. For teams with the resources to run Clay properly and targeting Western markets, Clay is worth the investment.
