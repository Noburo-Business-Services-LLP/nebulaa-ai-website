# Stop Writing Your Own Captions. Here's What to Do Instead.

There's a particular founder trap that nobody talks about. You know you need to build an audience. You've read the LinkedIn playbooks. You've seen other founders grow to 10,000 followers in 6 months.

So you open a blank document on Sunday night to write next week's posts. And you stare at it for 45 minutes.

You write a draft. Delete it. Write another one. Hate the opening. Close the tab. Tell yourself you'll do it tomorrow. You don't.

This is not a discipline problem. This is a priority problem with a technical solution.

## The Math Doesn't Work

Let's be honest about the numbers. Writing three LinkedIn posts per week, thoughtfully, with a hook, body, and CTA, takes most founders 60–90 minutes per post. That's 3–4.5 hours per week, 15–18 hours per month.

For a founder in the 0–1 phase, that time has a real cost. Three customer calls. One full day of product development. Thirty sales follow-ups.

The return on founder time spent on content is hard to measure. The return on founder time spent on customers is obvious.

## What You Should Actually Do

**Option 1: Hire a content writer.** Average cost: ₹15,000–25,000/month for someone decent. Problem: they don't know your voice. You'll spend half your time briefing them, editing their drafts, explaining the nuance. You end up doing 60% of the work anyway.

**Option 2: Use a content tool.** Every AI writing tool will generate bland, generic posts that sound like they were written by a robot trained on LinkedIn clichés. Your audience will notice immediately.
