# How a SaaS Founder in Bangalore Got 40 Warm Leads While on a Flight

Rohan had a problem most founders recognise immediately: he was spending 4 hours every week manually following up with leads from his website's contact form. Some leads would get a response within an hour. Others would wait three days. Most would go cold.

He discovered Nebulaa's Pulsar agent on a LinkedIn post. Decided to try the free trial. Set it up in an afternoon. And went on a business trip to Mumbai the next day.

When he landed — 40 leads had been contacted, qualified, and categorised. Twelve were marked "warm" and ready for a founder call.

## What Actually Happened

Here's the exact setup Rohan used:

**Step 1: The trigger**
Every time a new lead submitted the contact form, Pulsar was notified via webhook. This took 20 minutes to set up with a basic Zapier connection.

**Step 2: The qualification call**
Within 5 minutes of form submission, Pulsar called the lead from a local number. The script was personalised — it knew the company name, the product enquired about, and the lead's LinkedIn headline.

The call lasted between 90 seconds (uninterested) and 4 minutes (genuinely warm). Pulsar qualified them on three dimensions:
- Budget awareness
- Timeline (are they evaluating now or "just exploring")
- Decision authority

**Step 3: The follow-up sequence**
Uninterested leads went into a 30-day nurture WhatsApp sequence. Warm leads got a personalised email summary with booking link — directly to Rohan's calendar.
