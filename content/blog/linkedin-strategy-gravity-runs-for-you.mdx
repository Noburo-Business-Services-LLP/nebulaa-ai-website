# The 7-Step LinkedIn Strategy That Nebulaa Runs For You (That Would Take You 8 Hours/Week)

Most founders know they should be posting on LinkedIn. Almost none of them actually do it consistently. The reason isn't laziness — it's the invisible cost of the creative process.

Writing one post doesn't take 5 minutes. It takes 40. There's research, drafting, second-guessing, editing, formatting, scheduling. Do that three times a week and you've spent 2 hours you didn't have.

Nebulaa solves this. Here's exactly how.

## Step 1: Brand Voice Ingestion (Done Once)

The first thing Nebulaa does is read your website. Every page — home, about, product, blog. It extracts:
- Your vocabulary and tone
- The specific problems you solve
- The audience you write for
- How you talk about competitors

This takes about 60 seconds. After this, Nebulaa doesn't just know what you do. It writes like you.

## Step 2: ICP Mapping

Next, Nebulaa identifies your Ideal Customer Profile from your existing content. It cross-references this with LinkedIn audience data to understand what content format converts for your segment:
- Founders → story-driven, insight posts
- Marketers → tactical, framework posts
- SMB owners → result-driven, case-study posts

## Step 3: Competitor Intelligence

Nebulaa tracks the top 3 competitors you identify (or discovers them automatically from your website). Every day, it notes what they're posting, what topics they're targeting, and where there are content gaps.

This feeds the content calendar automatically.

## Step 4: The Daily Content Queue

Every morning, Nebulaa generates 3 posts:
- 1 thought leadership piece (LinkedIn long-form)
- 1 short-form hook (Twitter/X thread starter)
- 1 visual caption (Instagram)

Each post is uniquely written. No templates, no fill-in-the-blanks.

## Step 5: Festival & Trend Layer

Indian founders know that content tied to cultural moments performs significantly better. Budget Day analysis. IPL take. Diwali campaign. Nebulaa has a built-in calendar of 80+ Indian cultural moments per year and drafts content for each one — 3 days in advance.

## Step 6: Scheduling and Publishing

Once posts are in the queue, Nebulaa handles the rest. Optimal posting times are calculated per platform based on your audience's activity. Posts go live automatically. You get a morning summary of what went live.

## Step 7: Performance Learning

Over time, Nebulaa learns which of your posts get the most engagement and adjusts the content style accordingly. The longer you use it, the better it gets.
